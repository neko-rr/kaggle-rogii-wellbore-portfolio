This suggests that, at least in our case, optimizing for the Public LB led to worse generalization.

In contrast, our local cross-validation (OOF over all 773 training wells) tracked the Private LB much more closely. The model with the best CV also achieved the best Private score, even though it ranked worst on the Public LB.

One possible explanation is that the Public evaluation set had a distribution that differed from the Private evaluation set. If so, giving too much weight to models that were particularly strong on the Public LB may have amplified this bias instead of improving generalization.
