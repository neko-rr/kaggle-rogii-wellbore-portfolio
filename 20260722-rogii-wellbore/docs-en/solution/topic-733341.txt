After three months, ROGII - Wellbore Geology Prediction has closed. 6,191 teams and over 7,000 participants made 164,000 submissions at guessing where the drill bit sits inside the formation. Thank you all.

Congratulations to our top of the leaderboard: @w5833946 , @tatamikenn , @tereka &amp; @takoihiraokazu , and @alijs1 &amp; @arunodhayan &amp; @jsday96 &amp; @l1ghtsource - with @daimaruk right behind. (Standings are preliminary until results are verified.)

But the leaderboard is only half of it. The forums were exceptional! You taught each other - and taught us a few things about our own data. If you haven't posted your approach yet, please do; the negative results are as valuable as the wins.

Thank you to @ryanholbrook and @macruzbar, to the Kaggle team, and to everyone who prepared the data and kept the forums moving.

It's been a genuine pleasure hosting. See you in the next one.
