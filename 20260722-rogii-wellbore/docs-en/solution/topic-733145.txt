TLDR

Two channels with deliberately different failure modes, combined with frozen weights:



A physics/tabular channel 窶・structure-surface transfer + a momentum particle filter with a
deliberately weak GR likelihood, feeding a gradient-boosted ensemble.

A full-well neural channel 窶・three decoders over the complete well (a cost-volume CNN with
Viterbi decode, a GRU sequence decoder, an attention decoder), pre-blended 0.65/0.15/0.20.


final = 0.6658ﾂｷv31 + 0.4929ﾂｷleg40, then a per-row winsorisation of each leg to the blend ﾂｱ12 ft,
then a robust low-order polynomial projection per well.

Nothing here is exotic. What mattered was refusing to ship anything that couldn't clear a fold-safe
gate on 773 wells, for the six weeks that the public leaderboard said I was wrong.

Framing

Target is drift = TVT 竏・TVT_at_anchor, so predicting zero reproduces the flat baseline and
every model is measured against a meaningful zero.

The load-bearing identity is TVT = surface 竏・Z. Z is measured and therefore known everywhere,
including the eval zone 窶・so the entire high-frequency wiggle of TVT is free. The only hard part
is the smooth per-well surface trend. Any effort spent on per-station detail is spent in the wrong
place; I confirmed this on my own residuals before believing it (below).

Channel 1 窶・physics + tabular (CV 8.62)



Structure frames. Cluster the typewell master log, then transfer the marker-elevation surface
T = Z + TVT from neighbouring wells by anisotropic IDW and a kriging-lite fit.

Particle filter family. State is pos = TVT + Z, evolving as a momentum random walk under a
pointwise GR likelihood that is deliberately weak.

44 multi-scale log/trajectory features, into a LightGBM 5-seed bag + XGBoost + CatBoost.


The weak-GR choice is the single most counter-intuitive thing in the build. Every attempt to make
GR lead the estimate lost to a plain continuity prior 窶・nine separate experiments. Two other
teams reported the same result independently. The physical reason is in the acquisition: GR
coherence between the lateral and the typewell is usable at the heel (0.16窶・.50) and collapses to
0.02窶・.04 at the toe, which is exactly where the scored rows are. The exploitable signal is
continuity, not GR matching.

Channel 2 窶・full-well decoders (CV 7.64 blended)

Three decoders that consume the whole well as one sample rather than row-wise:



cost-volume CNN + Viterbi decode (weight 0.65) 窶・squared difference between the typewell GR
interpolated onto a TVT grid and the lateral GR per MD column, giving the classical geosteering
correlation panel; a CNN reads it and a Viterbi pass decodes a continuous track through it.

GRU sequence decoder (0.15), trained with an alignment/sharpness loss.

attention decoder (0.20).


They were chosen for disagreement, not accuracy. The seqdecode leg scores 8.85 standalone against
the CNN's 8.60, but it is more decorrelated (0.747) and beats the CNN on the big-drift wells, which
is where pooled RMSE is actually decided.

Frozen coefficients beat refitting. The frozen blend scores 7.6421; refitting the coefficients
per fold scores 7.6854. Refitting overfits the folds 窶・I left the v37-era weights untouched through
three subsequent leg changes.

The winsorisation (v42, and the one that scored)

Clip each leg to the blend ﾂｱ12 ft and recombine with the frozen coefficients. That's it 窶・no
selector (seven selector attempts had already failed). It fires on 8.6% of rows across 45 of 773
wells, de-shrinks the drift slope 0.598 竊・0.614, and improves the big-drift wells 22.71 竊・22.24.

Measured 竏・.062 on CV, 竏・.096 on public LB, 竏・.038 on a 200-well pseudo-test. Three independent
measurements, same sign 窶・which is why I trusted it despite it lowering my public score relative
to v40. It was worth 0.023 ft on private.

Three encoders agreeing that the per-well datum is not in the GR is not a modelling failure, it's
a measurement: gamma ray reports increments of rock character, not absolute depth, so the datum
is structurally a constant of integration. Dead reckoning cannot recover an unknown starting
position without an external reference.

I could always detect trouble 窶・model disagreement, particle spread, eval-zone length all rank
wells by expected error 窶・and never predict the direction of the correction. Seven selector
experiments died on exactly that gap.

What did not work

Kept deliberately, because most of the value of my log is in what's ruled out:



Per-well routing / selectors 窶・7 attempts, all negative. Including a self-limiting weight on
the weakest channel using its own evidence signals (竏・.019 against a 竏・.05 gate); explicit
monotone shrinkage preferred zero shrinkage.

Synthetic pretraining 窶・5 attempts. I built progressively more faithful forward simulators,
ending with a true 2D bedding field with an independent lateral facies component, calibrated to
reproduce the real heel竊稚oe coherence collapse (0.83竊・.37 against a real 0.81竊・.44). It landed at
the same wall. See "what I got wrong" below 窶・this is the one I misread.

Bigger models on the same inputs. A scaled full-well attention model decoded 9.70, worse
than the small attention leg (9.23) and the CNN (8.60).

Formation/geology sequence labelling. Dead on arrival: 54 of 60 eval zones never leave a
single formation, median 0 contact crossings, median 26 ft TVT span. This task is fine
positioning within one bed 窶・which rules out anything keyed to formation identity or facies.

Neighbour-well set encoders. The oracle looked like 竏・.98 ft until I ran the null: a random
single neighbour scores 33.51 against a truth-picked 9.46. The oracle was 24 ft of pure picking.
Never report an oracle without its random-choice null.

Transductive/online training on held-out wells' visible rows. Legal, and it worked on fold 0
(竏・.0506) 窶・then priced fold-safe across 5ﾃ・ runs at 竏・.0262, below gate.

Distilling the strongest public pipeline into a cheap leg: +0.0015. The student learns the
teacher's reproducible nuisance; residual correlation with truth 竏・.003.

L1-objective model diversity, heel-blended pseudo-typewell, adaptive blend weights,
leg-level winsorisation, per-well bias calibration. All sub-gate.

Two measurements made this concrete. First, my pipeline is not run-to-run reproducible 窶・
threaded GBM reductions give a reseed RMS of 0.306 ft, with one bistable well at 5.86 ft. Any
"improvement" smaller than that is not measurable, so the submit gate has to sit above the noise
floor, not above zero. Second, on a single day in July I evaluated six candidates, rejected all
six, and spent no submission. That is the process working.

The private leaderboard settled it. The public 6.40窶・.55 fork cluster (1,152 teams) landed at a
median private 9.458 and median rank 1,468; 10.6% of them medalled. Of the public top 500, 38%
kept any medal. Spearman(public, private) was 0.873 overall, but with a brutal tail 窶・one team went
public 5.406 竊・private 10.108.

If your CV is built on 773 wells and your public score is built on 52, the arithmetic is not
subtle. I still nearly talked myself out of it, and the thing that stopped me was
Tucker Arrants' post
showing single fold-models of one CV-5.13 ensemble scoring anywhere from 5.2 to 6.3 on public.

What I got wrong

Reading Tucker's 26th place solution
after the close: he had essentially the same representation I did 窶・well-as-image, TVT rows ﾃ・MD
columns, cost volume as an input channel, per-column softmax over depth with a soft-argmax readout.
The gap was not the idea. It was:



Scale. He hit "a wall around 7.5 CV" on a ResNet34 窶・my wall, the same number 窶・and broke it
with ImageNet-pretrained EffNetV2-XL/L2 backbones and 5 folds ﾃ・7 seeds. I measured
"more capacity doesn't help" at a few million parameters from scratch and generalised it upward.
That generalisation was wrong.

Synthetic pretraining, inverted. We both found that closing the synthetic竊排eal statistical
gap made things worse. I concluded the lever was dead and killed it. He concluded the knob
pointed the other way 窶・harder-than-real geology, cleaner-than-real GR correspondence, because
the synthetic world's job is to teach the skill, not to pass a statistical test against reality.
Same observation, opposite move, and his was right.


Two teams can reach identical negative results and only one of them has found the exit. Worth
remembering.
