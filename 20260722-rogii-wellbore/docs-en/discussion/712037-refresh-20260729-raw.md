Most public notebooks here fork one strong pipeline and cluster around ~7.2. Instead of adding another fork, I measured how much of the remaining error is even recoverable from the data we are given 窶・all on held-out wells.



The per-row target reduces to one number per well, a drift slope (the surface is ~99% linear).

That slope is not learnable from the legal features I tried on unseen fields (field-grouped OOF Rﾂｲ below zero, shuffle-controlled).

Why: GR carries real coarse signal (a particle filter gets ~16 ft down to ~10), but it can't pin the fine per-well slope through a legal calibration 窶・a measured degeneracy, not a property to chase.

Plus two traps that look like progress: the CV竊鱈B mirage and seed/refork variance.


It comes with a fork-and-go harness (oracle_ceiling + wall_test) that isn't wellbore-specific: drop your own out-of-fold predictions in, read your recoverable ceiling, and run the wall test 窶・leave-one-group-out with a shuffle-null control 窶・on your features.
